About this prototype

A schematic 3D map of the brachial and lumbosacral plexuses for teaching. Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, tap any node or nerve for details.

  • Explore: tap a nerve to highlight everything upstream (where its fibers come from) and downstream (what it feeds).
  • Lesion: tap nerves to injure them, or choose a clinical lesion. Red means every fiber passes through the lesion; amber means only some do (a partial deficit).
  • How “partial” is worked out: each structure lists the spinal levels it carries. A lesion removes any of those levels that pass through the injured point. It is a teaching approximation, not a measurement.
  • Shapes, lengths and spacing are schematic, not to scale. Real plexuses vary between people (prefixed and postfixed plexuses, communicating branches).
  • Written from standard anatomy. It has not been checked against your course textbook yet, so root values and a few branching patterns may differ from what you teach. See Review notes.
